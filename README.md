Agile Coaching Portfolio 🧭

A portfolio site built as an evidence file, not a resume — what I did, why, and what changed as a result, across four industries and thirteen years.

🔗 Live site: talktokvk-ship-it.github.io/kvk-agile-coaching

What this is

Most coaching portfolios list capabilities. This one is structured around outcomes: each capability is tied to what changed for a real team or organization, with the reasoning behind the approach made explicit rather than left implicit.

The core line running through the site: "I never told them the answer, I just made it impossible to miss." Every section — the leadership outcomes, the career arc, the redBus and Seamless case studies — is evidence for that same coaching philosophy, not a disconnected list of skills.

What's on the site
For Leadership — four outcomes framed specifically for the people making the hiring or engagement decision: delivery predictability, reduced escalation load, compressed decision cycles, and proof at scale
Career arc — a structured walk through four companies (redBus, Seamless Systems, S&P Global, Wells Fargo), each framed by what it added: where the philosophy was forged, where it met real scale, where it met regulation, where it met a global bank
Coaching philosophy — the systemic lens behind the work: a team's behavior is treated as a symptom of its structure, not its character
Detailed engagement evidence — CXO metrics negotiation at Seamless, product unification across 15+ client-specific versions into one with feature toggles, Big Room Planning at redBus with 12 engineering teams and direct CEO/CTO/CPO involvement
Design notes

Built on the same kraft/paper visual system as the companion Facilitation & Gamification Portfolio — cream backgrounds, sienna and moss-green accents — but structured as a denser, more analytical document, since this one is built for CXO and hiring-manager readers moving quickly through evidence rather than browsing techniques.

How it's built
Single-page site, no framework, no build step
Built through conversational prompting — I don't have a traditional engineering background; this was AI-assisted design and content work, structured around real coaching engagements and their outcomes
Deployed on GitHub Pages, directly from this repo
